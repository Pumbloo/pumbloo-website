Who we are

Pumbloo Interactive is a sole proprietorship run by Taran Tushar Chindarkar, based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. We are the "data fiduciary" (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) and the "controller" (EU and UK GDPR) for the data described here.

What we collect

We collect only what we need:

  • Basic server logs. Our host, GitHub Pages, automatically records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use this to identify you.
  • Your cookie choice. Saved in your own browser so we don't ask again. See the cookie policy.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us or use the support form (which opens your own email app), we receive your name, email address and whatever you write.
  • Analytics, only if you agree. If you click "Accept" (or tick Analytics) on the cookie banner, Google Analytics counts pages visited, rough location (country or city), device type and how you reached us. IP addresses are not stored, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and we don't let Google use this data for its own products. If you don't agree, Google Analytics is never loaded.

We don't collect payment details, ID documents or sensitive data, and the login page doesn't store anything yet.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use a few services that may process it for us:

  • GitHub (Microsoft) for hosting the website.
  • Google for email, and for Google Analytics only if you consent.
  • YouTube (Google) only when you load a video.
  • Discord, Instagram, X, YouTube and itch.io if you follow a link to them. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Some of these companies store data outside India, for example in the USA. They use standard safeguards for international transfers. We may also share data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Emails and support messages: up to 2 years after the conversation ends, then deleted.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months, if enabled.
  • Your cookie choice: until you clear it or 12 months pass.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we have about you and get a copy,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it,
  • withdraw consent (for example to analytics) at any time, which is as easy as giving it,
  • nominate someone to act for you if you can't (India).

You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Board of India or your local authority in the EU or UK. We'd appreciate the chance to fix things first.

Delete my data

To have everything we hold about you deleted, email hello@pumbloo.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the email address you used with us, or use the support form and choose "Delete my data". We'll confirm within 30 days. To remove what this site saved in your own browser, use the button on the cookie policy page.

Children

This website isn't aimed at children under 13. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you send us any personal information. If we learn we have a child's data without that permission, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, such as starting to sell games or opening accounts, we'll make it clear on the home page.

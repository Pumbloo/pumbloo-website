Small studio, big world

Pumbloo Interactive is an independent game studio based in India. We create stylised low-poly worlds, modelled in Blender and built in Unity.

Our long-term goal is an open-world fantasy game with a top-down perspective. We're starting with smaller projects to build our tools, our craft and our community along the way.

  • NowPrototyping environments and building our first playable scenes.
  • NextA compact top-down game, planned for release on itch.io.
  • Follow alongDevelopment updates on YouTube, Instagram and Discord.
